<h3><strong>Definition of homework</strong></h3>
<p>Homework refers to any learning activity that students are required by
the school to complete outside of curriculum time. The mode of delivery
can be pen-and-paper or online tasks. This includes assignments and projects.</p>
<h3><strong>Objective of homework</strong></h3>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To reinforce students’ learning, provide feedback on their progress and
cultivate a healthy disposition towards continual learning</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To develop good study habits, encourage ownership of independent learning.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To provide timely information on learning progress</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To allow teachers to consolidate learning that has taken place in class
and to check for students’ understanding and progress so as to provide
timely feedback; intervention and support; and review the effectiveness
of the lessons.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Working together</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Students are encouraged to</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; understand the purpose
of homework and what is expected of them.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; keep track of homework
instructions and deadlines in the student diary daily.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; manage their time well
to ensure homework is completed and submitted on time.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; give their best effort
in completing homework.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; review feedback from
teachers on homework submitted and act on it.</p>
<p><s>&nbsp;</s>
</p>
<p><strong>Parents can support by:</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Creating a home environment
conducive to studying and the completion of homework.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Guiding their child
in managing workload, meeting deadlines and prioritising their time among
school and out-of-school activities.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Reminding their child
to complete school homework first before other activities.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Reinforcing good study
habits and attitudes.</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Working in partnership
with the school and teachers to support their child's learning and development.</p>
