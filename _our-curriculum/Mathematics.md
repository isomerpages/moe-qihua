<h3><strong>Vision</strong></h3>
<p>Every Qihuaian, an engaged Mathematics learner equipped with problem solving
skills and Mathematical concepts.</p>
<h3><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Our Programmes enable our students to:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>develop a positive attitude towards Mathematics.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>acquire the essential Mathematical concepts and skills for real-world
situation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>develop the ability to reason logically and communicate Mathematics.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>harness Mathematical tools and technology in the learning and application
of Mathematics.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Programmes to ENABLE:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>ICAN (Improving Confidence and Achievement in Numeracy): levels up confidence
and achievement of low progress students.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Learning Support for Mathematics (P1 – P2): provides early support for
Primary One and Two students who have weak basic numeracy skills during
Mathematics lessons in smaller groups. The programme focuses on basic Mathematics
skills and concepts to build good foundation in students.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>MBL (Mastery Based Learning) (P1 – P4): provides support for students
who have weak basic numeracy after school.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Programmes to ENGAGE:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Structured Thinking Skills and Heuristics (P1 – P6): ensures progressions
and continuity in the teaching of problem-solving skills.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Mathematics Literacy and Communication: provides opportunities for engagement
in investigate work that develop the thinking, reasoning and communication
skills.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>SSM (Sustained Support for Mathematics): provides hands-on activities
which develop conceptual understanding of Mathematics.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>E-Learning: provides opportunities for self-directed learning.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Programmes to ENRICH:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Mathematics Olympiad Training (P3 – P6): enriches the learning experience
of high progress students.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Mathematics Competitions: provides opportunities for students to identify
and explore their talent in Mathematics.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Curriculum Innovation</strong>
</p>
<p>TEMO (Teach and Explain Mathematics Ourselves):</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To develop students’&nbsp;Mathematical reasoning and communication skills
using Mathematics language.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To provide students metacognitive experience by</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>encouraging students to think aloud the strategies and methods they use
to solve particular problems.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>encouraging students to seek alternative ways of solving the same problem
and to check the appropriateness and reasonableness of the answer.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>allowing students to discuss how to solve a particular problem and to
explain the different methods that they use for solving the problem.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
